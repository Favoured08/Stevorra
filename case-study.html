01

Executive Support

The Challenge

A business executive based in the UK, running three separate businesses at once, needed hands-on support executing tasks in Nigeria — but had no one she trusted enough to hand things to.

Our Approach & Execution

Stepped in as a dedicated executive assistant for a two-week engagement. Coordinated outstanding tasks, managed communication with vendors, and kept her updated for quick decisions despite time differences.

The Outcome

Her businesses in Nigeria were established and operating. Momentum that had been stalled by distance was fully restored.

02

Confidential Representation

The Challenge

A client living abroad needed to travel home for a family event, but for personal reasons did not want relatives to know. She needed someone she could trust with complete discretion and financial accountability.

Our Approach & Execution

Took on the role of her on-the-ground representative. Sourced fabrics, coordinated vendors, managed every purchase, and provided ongoing updates—continuing to source items even after she returned abroad.

The Outcome

The visit went exactly as planned, with complete discretion maintained and every expense fully documented and accounted for.

03

Large-Scale Event Operations

The Challenge

Managing the bulk of the operational load — procurement, vendor sourcing, and budget accountability — for a church anniversary programme involving over 200 people.

Our Approach & Execution

Took ownership of procurement directly. Sourced and procured branded shirts for 200+ attendees with zero shortfalls, oversaw market runs, and managed reporting throughout.

The Outcome

The programme ran smoothly with full accountability for every item procured and every naira spent — nothing missing, nothing unaccounted for.

Risk Mitigation

How We Protect You

For the client who needs more than a good story before handing over something that matters. Here's exactly how we reduce your risk.

A Team, Not a Single Point of Contact

Every engagement is assigned a dedicated lead — backed by a team. If your point of contact is unavailable, your project doesn't stall. Continuity is built into how we operate.

Contracts Before Anything Begins

Every engagement starts with a clear, written scope of work and a signed confidentiality agreement — before any task, payment, or site visit takes place.

An Audit Trail for Everything

Every purchase has a receipt. Every site visit has a report. Every recommendation is in writing. There's a documented answer for everything, not a memory to rely on.

If Something Goes Wrong, You Hear It First

Weekly reporting exists so issues surface early. If something doesn't go to plan, we tell you directly, alongside what we're doing about it to course-correct immediately.

How do I know my money is being spent correctly?

Every purchase is documented with receipts, and significant spending requires your approval before it happens. You'll also receive regular reports with photos or video so you can see, not just hear, what's been done.

Can I use Stevorra for a one-off task, or only ongoing work?

Both. Some engagements are one-time — a single verification, a specific purchase, a one-off property fix. Others are ongoing, like routine property oversight or a contract-based corporate engagement.

How do you handle discretion and confidentiality?

Discretion is one of our two most important values, alongside integrity. Details of your engagement are shared only with the people directly involved in delivering it, and only to the extent needed.

I'm not in Nigeria. How will I know what's happening?

You'll have a dedicated point of contact and a clear reporting schedule — typically weekly — with written updates, photos, and video where relevant.
