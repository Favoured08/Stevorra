Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers before you begin.

Good representation starts with clear expectations. Here are answers to the questions clients ask most often.

Ask Us Directly →
What exactly does Stevorra do?

Stevorra provides trusted on-ground representation and operational support for individuals, professionals, businesses, and property owners who need reliable assistance when they cannot be physically present.

Who can use Stevorra?

Our services are designed for diaspora clients, international clients, executives, professionals, businesses, organisations, and property owners who require trusted support on the ground.

Can you make decisions on my behalf?

We can act within the scope and authority you provide. Major decisions, approvals, and agreed expenditures remain subject to your instructions.

How do I know what is happening?

We provide regular updates appropriate to the nature of your assignment. These may include written reports, photographs, videos, receipts, and other documentation.

Do you handle payments?

Where payment handling is part of an agreed assignment, the process, limits, and approval requirements are established in advance.

Can you work on an ongoing basis?

Yes. Depending on your needs, Stevorra can provide one-time assignments, recurring support, or ongoing representation.

How do I get started?

Simply tell us what you need. We'll discuss your requirements, clarify the scope, and explain how we can help.
